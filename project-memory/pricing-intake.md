## Load tiers and starting prices

| Tier | Starting price |
|------|---------------|
| Minimum Load | $125 |
| 1/4 Load | $175 |
| 1/2 Load | $350 |
| 3/4 Load | $525 |
| Full Load (12×8×5 dump trailer) | $699 |

## What's included in every price

- All labor
- All disposal / dump fees
- Taxes included
- No hidden fees

## Surcharges

- Appliances: +$30 each (disposal surcharges)

## Items not accepted

Large quantities of hazardous materials: paint, chemicals, propane, fertilizers, pressure-treated wood.

## Minimum fee

Yes, a fixed minimum applies regardless of load size. Dump fees are baked into the tier price.

## Items that trigger surcharges (beyond appliances)

Tires, appliances (fridges, washers), construction debris (concrete, drywall), electronics/e-waste, extremely heavy items (pianos, hot tubs).

## Distance impact on price

Moderate. (Matt rated it 2/5.)

## Job-difficulty factors

| Factor | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Stairs / multi-floor access | Moderate surcharge |
| Tight or difficult access | Minor surcharge |
| Items needing 2+ team members | Minor surcharge |
| Disassembly required (sheds, large furniture) | Significant surcharge |

## Same-day / rush jobs

A percentage increase is applied.

## Quoting process

- First questions Matt asks: photo of items, city, when they need it done.
- From photos, he weighs volume (most critical) and material type (to identify surcharges).
- Final price differs from estimate **15-30% of the time**. Usual cause: customer adds more items, or the pile is larger than the photo showed.
- In-person visit required for complex cleanouts or construction sites.
- Hard quote vs range: depends on job size and complexity.

## Competitive positioning

- Main competitors: 1-800-GOT-JUNK and other national franchises; local independent haulers.
- Turnaround: within 2-3 days.
- Differentiators (Matt's own selection): better communication and responsiveness, cleaner/more professional work ethic, more comprehensive removal services including specialized demolition, personalized/local experience.

## Customer FAQs and misconceptions

- Top pre-booking questions: "What is your pricing?" and "How soon can you come?"
- Most common misconception: that pricing is a fixed flat rate.
- When customers ghost after a quote, Matt's read: price was too high.
