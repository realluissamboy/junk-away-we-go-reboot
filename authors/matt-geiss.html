Owner · Author

Matt Geiss

Owner, Junk Away We Go Demolition Inc.

Matt founded Junk Away We Go in 2020 after more than a decade working across construction, site management, and logistics in Southern California. He started the company with one truck, a dump trailer, and a simple idea: post the prices, show up on time, and leave the job site cleaner than you found it.

Today Matt runs every job personally out of Poway. When you text for a quote, Matt is the one reading the photos. When the crew pulls up in the driveway, Matt is usually on the tailgate. It's the difference between a small local operation and a national franchise, and it's why he prefers to keep it that way.

Junk Away We Go holds CA Contractor's License #1128050 and carries full general liability and workers' compensation insurance.
