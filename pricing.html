What bumps the price

A few things cost more, and we'll tell you before the job:

  • arrow_rightAppliances: +$30 each (disposal surcharge)
  • arrow_rightTires, construction debris, e-waste: priced case by case
  • arrow_rightStairs, tight access, disassembly, pianos, hot tubs: added for real effort
  • arrow_rightSame-day / rush: percentage premium, quoted up front
block

What we don't take

We're not set up for hazardous materials in large quantities:

  • closePaint, solvents, household chemicals
  • closePropane tanks, fuel, pesticides
  • closePressure-treated wood in bulk

One old can of paint in a garage cleanout is fine. Fifteen cans is a hazmat job, and the county has a free drop-off for that.

support_agent

How we quote

Text us a photo, your city, and when you need it gone. Most of the time we quote the tier right there. For complex cleanouts (estate jobs, hoarder situations, full construction tear-outs), we come look in person first. About 15 to 30% of the time the final price lands higher than the text quote, almost always because the pile in person is bigger than the photo. We tell you the new number before we load another thing.
