When paying us makes more sense than DIY

California's Bye Bye Mattress program recycles mattresses for free if you can drop them off. Your city trash hauler does one or two free bulky pickups a year. If either works for you, do that; it's cheap, and the fee's already baked into the price you paid for the mattress. We made the case for it in our full mattress disposal guide. But here's when calling us is actually the right move:

schedule

You need it gone this week

Bulky pickup programs book 2-4 weeks out. Drop-off sites require time and a truck. We show up in a day or two.

stairs

It's not on the ground floor

Third-floor walkups, loft beds, awkward staircases. We come inside and get it.

bed

Multiple mattresses

One is cheap at the drop-off. Four from a tenant turnover, plus frames and furniture, is better bundled into one load with us.

block

It doesn't qualify for the free programs

Wet, disassembled, bedbug, or burned mattresses are refused by Bye Bye Mattress and most city haulers. We take them anyway.

How it works

  1. 1Text us a photo of the mattress (and any other items), your zip code, and when you'd like it gone.
