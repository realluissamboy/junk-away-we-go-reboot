What the quote covers

water_drop

Draining

We can handle the drain on site. If you've already drained it, the job is cheaper and goes faster.

power

Electrical disconnect

We disconnect the whip at the tub side once you confirm the breaker is off. We don't pull the subpanel side; that's an electrician's job.

construction

Disassembly

Most tubs won't fit through a side gate whole. We cut with a reciprocating saw, bundle the pieces, keep the foam mess contained.

fence

Fence panel access

If we need to pull fence panels to get the tub out, we do. We put them back when the job is done.

local_shipping

Hauling

Shell to the dump. Motor, pump, and copper plumbing to scrap metal. Everything metal gets recycled, not landfilled.

cleaning_services

Cleanup

The pad or deck where the tub sat should look like the tub was never there. No foam shreds, no splinters.

What moves the price

Access and path out

add
The biggest variable. A 4-foot side gate straight to the driveway is cheap. A built-in deck with a narrow 30-inch gate means more disassembly and longer loading time.

Size

add
Anything bigger than a standard 7x7 footprint, or swim-spa hybrids, run at the higher end of the range.

Cover, surround, or gazebo

add
A vinyl cover is free. A built-in cedar surround or a gazebo adds demolition time ($150 to $400 depending on scope).

Drained & disconnected before we arrive

add
Drained tubs, with the breaker off for 24 hours, save 1 to 2 hours of on-site time. That's reflected in the lower end of the range.

Same-day or rush

add
Possible when the tub is already drained and the breaker is off. Rush jobs carry a percentage premium, quoted on the first text.
