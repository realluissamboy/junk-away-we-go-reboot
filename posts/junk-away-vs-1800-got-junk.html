Junk Away We Go vs. 1-800-GOT-JUNK: what actually differs

By Matt Geiss April 17, 2026 5 min read

Almost every customer who reaches out to us has already looked at 1-800-GOT-JUNK. Fair enough. They're on TV, they have a memorable number, they've been doing this since before half of San Diego had WiFi. Here's an honest look at how we actually differ, and when one makes more sense than the other.

The short version

1-800-GOT-JUNK is a national franchise. When you call, you reach a central booking line. A local franchisee does the work. They give you a window, show up with a uniformed crew in a branded truck, and hand you a total at the end.

Junk Away We Go is a Poway-based family operation. Matt picks up the phone. Matt quotes the job. Matt usually shows up. The pricing is posted on our website, the dump fees are already in the number, and the whole thing moves faster because there's no call center layer.

Pricing: posted vs on-site reveal

This is the biggest difference people feel.

1-800-GOT-JUNK does not publish pricing on their website. Their policy is that they give you a final number on-site, after they see the load. There's a reason for that, it protects the franchisee from undercutting themselves over the phone. But it also means you don't know what it costs until they're standing in your driveway.

Us: our tiers are on the site. Minimum load $125. Quarter load $175. Half $350. Three-quarter $525. Full dump trailer $699. Dump fees and taxes included. Surcharges for appliances ($30 each) and a handful of heavy or hazardous categories that cost us more at the dump.

Net effect: you can text us a photo and have a real number in five minutes, without a truck coming out.

Turnaround

1-800-GOT-JUNK typically books two to seven days out in most San Diego zip codes, depending on crew availability. Same-day is possible but usually has a premium and requires the right franchise location to have a gap in the schedule.

We turn most jobs around in 2 to 3 days. Rush and same-day jobs carry a percentage premium, same as anyone, but we'll give you that number on the first text instead of after a call-center transfer.

Who actually answers the phone

With 1-800-GOT-JUNK, the first person you talk to is a booker in a shared call center. They take the job, pass it to the local franchise, and the crew you actually meet is third-in-line on the context chain. Quality of that handoff varies by franchise.

With us, the person quoting the job, scheduling the job, and loading the trailer is often the same guy. That has its own tradeoffs, Matt isn't on the phone 24/7, and the crew is small. But when you're mid-text and you need to change the job from a half load to a full, nothing gets lost.

Scale and what that buys

1-800-GOT-JUNK has more trucks, more crews, and more geographic coverage. If you need eight trucks on a commercial job next Tuesday, call them. If you're in La Jolla on a Saturday at 4pm and we're booked, they probably have someone available.

What scale doesn't give you is the thing most homeowners actually want, which is a real person who remembers your job and prices it honestly.

Demolition

1-800-GOT-JUNK does light junk removal, appliance haul, garage cleanouts, the standard list. They don't do demolition.

We do both. Shed teardowns, deck removal, interior wall demolition, hot tub extraction, concrete slab breakout on smaller jobs. If your project is actually "tear this out and haul it away," we can usually do the whole thing in one trip with one crew instead of hiring a demo guy and a haul guy separately.

Where we differ on the ground

These are the four things Matt hears from first-time customers who came from 1-800-GOT-JUNK:

  • Communication. Texts get answered in minutes, not hours. You talk to the guy doing the job, not a middle layer.
  • Cleanup. We sweep after. The driveway and the room look done when we leave.
  • Flexibility. If you add a bookcase five minutes before we pull away, we'll grab it and adjust the tier if needed. Nobody has to call dispatch.
  • Local. Matt has been in Poway a long time. He knows the streets, the HOAs, the apartment complex dumpster rules, the Saturday traffic on Pomerado. It saves time.

When 1-800-GOT-JUNK is the right call

It's a fair tool for certain jobs. If any of these are true, they're worth calling:

  • You need a very large crew on a very tight deadline.
  • Your company requires a national vendor with specific insurance paperwork we'd need a few days to match.
  • You're outside our service area and same-day matters more than price.

For most San Diego homeowners and small businesses, though, we'll be faster, cheaper, and easier to work with. And you'll know the number before anyone shows up.

Try us first. If it's not a fit, we'll say so.

Send a photo and a zip code. We'll come back with a real tier number and a window. If the job is genuinely not in our lane, we'll tell you who to call instead.
