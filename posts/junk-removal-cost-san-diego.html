How much does junk removal cost in San Diego?

By Matt Geiss April 17, 2026 6 min read

Most people call us expecting a runaround. A number they can't pin down, hidden fees on the invoice, "well, it depends" on everything. That's not how we work. Here is what a junk removal job actually costs in San Diego in 2026, and what changes the price.

Our starting prices

We price by how much space your stuff takes up in the truck. Our full trailer is 12 feet long, 8 feet wide, 5 feet tall. That is the whole sandbox.

Load size Starts at
Minimum load$125
1/4 load$175
1/2 load$350
3/4 load$525
Full load (12x8x5 dump trailer)$699

"Starts at" means exactly that. These are the numbers you begin from, and we'll tell you straight up if your job bumps past a tier before we load anything.

What's included

Every price on that list already covers:

  • Labor. Two guys, the truck, the trailer, the time.
  • Dump fees. Most companies add these at the end. We don't.
  • Taxes. The quote is the total.

No truck roll fee. No fuel surcharge. No "oh by the way" on the invoice.

What bumps the price up

A few things legitimately cost more, and we'd rather tell you before the job than fight about it after.

Items that carry a surcharge

These items cost the dump extra, so they cost you extra:

  • Appliances (refrigerators, washers, dryers, ovens): +$30 each
  • Tires, construction debris, electronics, and e-waste: priced case by case based on volume and material
  • Extremely heavy items like pianos and hot tubs

Access and effort

The dump trailer is parked on the street. The further your stuff is from the trailer, the longer the job, the more it costs. Specifically:

  • Stairs or multi-floor access: moderate bump
  • Tight hallways, basements, narrow side yards: minor bump
  • Items that need two people to move (big dressers, gun safes, marble countertops): minor bump
  • Disassembly required (sheds, large playsets, bolted-down furniture): significant bump, since that's real carpentry time on top of the haul

Distance

We're based in Poway and we cover most of San Diego County. If you're out past our normal service area, there's a modest drive-time add. If you're 30+ miles out, we'll tell you the exact add before we book.

Same-day or rush jobs

Yes, we do them. They run at a percentage premium over the standard rate. If you need it gone today, say so up front and we'll quote the rush number on the first text.

What we won't take

We're not set up for hazardous materials in meaningful quantities. That means no paint, no chemicals, no pesticides, no pressure-treated wood, no propane tanks. One can of old paint in a garage cleanout is fine. Fifteen cans is a hazmat job. The county has drop-off programs for that stuff and we'll point you to the nearest one.

How we quote (and when it changes)

Text us. Send a few photos. Tell us what city you're in and when you want it gone. Most of the time we can give you a tier estimate right there.

About 15 to 30% of the time, the final price lands higher than the initial text quote. Almost always because the pile in person is bigger than the pile in the photo, or because you found more stuff you wanted gone while we were there. We'll tell you the new number before we load another thing.

For complex cleanouts (full estate jobs, hoarder situations, construction tear-outs), we'll come look in person before quoting. It's a 20-minute drive that saves everyone from guessing.

Why customers ghost, and what we do about it

When someone gets a quote from us and disappears, it's almost always because they got a cheaper number from a guy with a pickup on Craigslist. Sometimes that works out for them. Sometimes the guy doesn't show, or shows up without a truck big enough, or dumps the load in a canyon and the homeowner gets the fine. We can't compete with $60 cash if that's the bar. What we can do is show up on time, haul the whole pile, and hand you an invoice that matches the quote. That's the pitch.

Ready to get a real number on your job?

Send us a photo and the zip code. We'll text you back with a tier, the add-ons if any apply, and a window for the haul.
