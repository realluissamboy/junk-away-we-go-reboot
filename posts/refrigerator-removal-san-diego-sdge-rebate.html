Old fridge? SDG&E pays $75 to take it.

By Matt Geiss April 17, 2026 5 min read

Most San Diego homeowners don't know this: if your old fridge still works, SDG&E will pick it up for free and pay you about $75 to let them recycle it. Same for a working freezer, except that one pays $35. The rebate is real, the pickup is real, and the program has been running for years. Here's exactly how to get it and what to do when you don't qualify.

The SDG&E Appliance Recycling Program, in plain English

SDG&E runs an appliance recycling program through a partner called ARCA Recycling. The point, from SDG&E's side, is to get inefficient old fridges and freezers out of garages and second kitchens. Old units burn way more electricity than modern ones. Getting them off the grid is cheaper for SDG&E than adding supply. So they pay you to get rid of them.

How much they pay

  • Refrigerator: $75
  • Standalone freezer: $35
  • Room air conditioner (window unit): $25, limit two per year

Check the current SDG&E rebate amounts before you assume; they update.

Who qualifies

  • You have to be an SDG&E residential electric customer.
  • The unit has to be working when they pick it up. They plug it in on the truck to confirm.
  • The unit has to be between 10 and 30 cubic feet for a fridge, or 10 to 30 cubic feet for a freezer.
  • You have to own the unit (renters can participate with landlord approval).
  • Limit two appliances per household per year.

How to schedule

Go to the SDG&E rebate page linked above and click through to the appliance recycling form. You'll enter your address, unit type, and a pickup window. ARCA schedules the pickup, usually within 2 to 4 weeks depending on demand. They call 24 hours ahead to confirm.

When it doesn't work for you

A few situations where the SDG&E program won't help:

  • Your fridge is broken. They won't take it. This is the most common reason people end up calling us instead.
  • It's too small (mini-fridge, dorm fridge). Those don't qualify.
  • You need it gone this week. The SDG&E pickup is typically 2 to 4 weeks out. Moving day tomorrow doesn't fit.
  • It's in an inaccessible location. ARCA's crew will haul from a garage or ground floor only. Upstairs laundry room with a fridge? They won't do stairs. We will.
  • You're in a rental and the landlord won't sign off.
  • You've already done two this year.

What we charge if the rebate isn't an option

Appliance removal through us runs the load tier cost plus a $30 appliance surcharge because disposal facilities charge us extra to take units with refrigerant. A single fridge in an otherwise empty load is the minimum tier ($125) plus the surcharge, so $155 total. If you've got a stove, microwave, or other appliances coming out at the same time, add them to the same tier; the surcharge only applies per refrigerant-containing appliance.

Our full pricing breakdown covers the tier logic.

The legal part (because California is strict about this)

Refrigerators, freezers, and window AC units in the US before 2010 contain HCFC refrigerant. Federal law (Clean Air Act Section 608) requires certified technicians to capture refrigerant before the unit is crushed or landfilled. It is illegal to drag an old fridge to the dump yourself without handling the refrigerant. It's also illegal to leave one on the curb in most San Diego County jurisdictions.

When we haul an appliance, we route it to a certified appliance recycler that handles refrigerant capture correctly. That's one of the reasons for the $30 surcharge. The alternative, curb dumping or informal disposal, is a $500 to $5,000 fine range depending on the city.

Quick decision tree

  • Fridge works, 10-30 cu ft, you're SDG&E, and you can wait 2 to 4 weeks: free pickup + $75 rebate from SDG&E.
  • Fridge is broken, too small, or you need it gone sooner: $155 through us (minimum load + appliance surcharge).
  • Fridge plus a bunch of other stuff coming out: bigger load tier, same $30 surcharge per appliance.

One small tip

If you're going to do the SDG&E program, keep the fridge plugged in until the morning of pickup. An empty, clean, unplugged fridge that's been sitting for three weeks grows a smell that nobody wants to deal with. Also, take the glass shelves out and tape the doors shut. ARCA's checklist asks for both.

Ready to get it out?

If the SDG&E program works for you, that's the cheap play, we recommend it. If it doesn't, we'll take it and handle disposal properly.
