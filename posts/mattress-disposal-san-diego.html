How to dispose of a mattress in San Diego

By Matt Geiss April 17, 2026 7 min read

Mattresses are one of the harder household items to get rid of legally in California. Regular trash won't take them. The dump charges extra by the pound. And dumping one on the curb gets you a fine. The good news: you probably have three real options, at least one of them is free, and you already paid for it whether you know it or not.

Option 1: Free drop-off through Bye Bye Mattress

California runs a statewide mattress recycling program called Bye Bye Mattress, managed by the Mattress Recycling Council. Every time you buy a new mattress or box spring in California, you pay a recycling fee (about $10.50 per unit, baked into the sale). That fee funds this program. Dropping off an old mattress at a participating site in San Diego County costs you nothing, because you've already paid for it.

Drop-off sites include several waste transfer stations and recycling centers across the county. You show up, drop it off, leave. No appointment needed at most locations.

What they take

  • Mattresses and box springs of any size (twin through California king)
  • Conventional innerspring, foam, latex, and hybrid
  • Up to a reasonable household quantity

What they won't take

  • Wet mattresses. If it rained on your way over, they'll turn you away.
  • Infested mattresses. Bedbugs, fleas, mold.
  • Burned or heavily soiled.
  • Disassembled mattresses. They want it whole.
  • Futons, sleeper-sofa mattresses, waterbeds, crib mattresses.

If your mattress fails any of those, skip to Option 3.

The catch: you need a vehicle that can fit a mattress, and you need time during the drop-off site's operating hours. Which rules out a lot of people.

Option 2: Bulky-item pickup from your trash service

Most San Diego County residents are entitled to at least one free bulky-item pickup per year through their trash hauler. The specifics vary by city:

  • City of San Diego (Environmental Services Department): 1 free pickup per household per year, includes mattresses and box springs. Schedule through the city's online form.
  • Poway, Escondido, San Marcos, parts of North County: EDCO handles these. Residential customers typically get 2 free bulky pickups per year. Call EDCO or log into your account to schedule.
  • Oceanside, Vista, Carlsbad: Waste Management. Free bulky pickup usually requires scheduling a week or two out.

You leave the mattress at the curb the night before pickup. They haul it in the regular truck or a dedicated one.

The catches:

  • Wait time. Busy seasons (spring, post-holidays) book out 2 to 4 weeks.
  • One free pickup usually means one event. If you have a mattress plus a couch plus a dresser, they may charge for the extras or limit the load.
  • Some programs require the item to be out at the curb by 6 AM, which can be annoying if you've got an HOA or a short trash-day window.

Option 3: Pay someone to haul it

The honest case for junk removal on a single mattress: it's $125 at our minimum load. That's more than zero. So when does it make sense?

  • You're moving this week and the city can't pick up for another two weeks.
  • You don't have a truck and neither do your friends, and renting a U-Haul to drive to a drop-off site costs about the same as just paying us.
  • Multiple mattresses. Estate cleanout, tenant turnover, Airbnb refresh. The per-mattress cost drops fast at $125 minimum for up to a couple, or $175 for a full quarter load with additional items.
  • Your mattress doesn't qualify for Bye Bye Mattress (wet, infested, disassembled). The city bulky program has the same restrictions. Junk removal takes it anyway.
  • You need it gone today. Same-day service carries a rush premium, but it exists.

What not to do

Don't leave it on the curb on a random day hoping someone takes it. In San Diego County, unauthorized bulky items left on the curb can result in citations starting at $100 per item and climbing to $1,000 for repeat incidents. The city does track this. And neighbors do call.

Don't cut it up and put it in the bin. Most haulers won't take cut mattresses, and trying to hide the foam in your weekly trash gets flagged.

Quick decision guide

  • Got time, got a truck, mattress is clean: Bye Bye Mattress. Free.
  • Got time, don't have a truck: schedule bulky pickup. Free or cheap.
  • Need it gone this week, multiple mattresses, or can't drop off for any reason: call a junk removal crew (us or someone else). Starts at $125.
