Local stuff that actually matters

Poway's free bulky-item pickup

EDCO, the residential hauler for most of Poway, gives you 2 free bulky-item pickups per year. For a single mattress or a couch that can wait 2 to 4 weeks, that's a good option and we'll tell you to use it. For anything bigger or faster, call us.

The Poway Road traffic problem

We schedule around the morning and evening crunches so the truck isn't sitting in front of your house on the clock. If you need a specific arrival window, say so up front and we'll work backward from there.

HOA rules in the newer developments

Some Poway HOAs don't allow dumpsters or trailers parked at the curb overnight. We work same-day so the trailer never sits. If your HOA requires a certificate of insurance before we set foot on the property, mention it when you text and we'll have it emailed before we arrive.
