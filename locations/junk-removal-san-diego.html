San Diego stuff worth knowing

The city's free bulky pickup

City of San Diego Environmental Services gives each household one free bulky-item pickup per year. If you have a single item (one mattress, one couch) and you can wait 2 to 4 weeks, schedule through the city's bulky pickup form. Free beats $125. For anything larger or faster, call us.

Parking and the side-street access problem

Older SD neighborhoods (North Park, South Park, Hillcrest, Golden Hill) were laid out for Model Ts. Our trailer is 12 feet long on a 3/4-ton truck. If your street is narrow, a permit-parking zone, or has a street-sweep schedule that morning, flag it when you text. We'll plan for it.

HOA & condo requirements

Downtown highrises and Mission Valley condos often require a certificate of insurance (COI) and proof of licensure before work. Mention your building when you text and we'll email the COI before we arrive. Some buildings also require service-elevator reservations; give us lead time.
