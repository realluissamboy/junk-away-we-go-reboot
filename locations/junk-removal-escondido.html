Escondido stuff worth knowing

EDCO bulky item pickup

EDCO covers most of Escondido residential. They offer 2 free bulky item pickups per year to residential customers. If it's one mattress or one couch and you can wait a week or two, schedule through EDCO first. Save the $125 for the bigger jobs.

Fire zone disposal

Parts of East Escondido and the backcountry are in the CAL FIRE SRA (State Responsibility Area). Brush clearance, defensible-space debris, old wood fencing — we take it and route it to the county green waste facility. If you're under a 100-foot clearance notice from CAL FIRE, we can work fast to help you come into compliance.

HOA and gated communities

Hidden Meadows, Champagne Village, the Country Club. Most gated communities require a COI (certificate of insurance) naming the HOA as additional insured before crews enter. Give us the HOA name and we'll have the COI emailed ahead of arrival.
