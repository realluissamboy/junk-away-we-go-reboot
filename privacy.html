Effective date: April 17, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Junk Away We Go Demolition Inc. ("we", "us") collects when you visit our website or contact us, how we use it, and the rights you have over it. We wrote it in plain language because nothing about privacy should be confusing.

What we collect

Information you give us directly

  • Contact form submissions on this site. If you fill out our form, we receive the name, email address, phone number, and message you provide, along with any photos or details about your job.
  • Direct messages. If you email, text, or call us, we keep a record of the conversation so we can quote accurately and follow up.

Information we receive automatically

  • Standard web server data from our hosting provider: your IP address, browser type, pages visited, referring site, and timestamps. This is used to keep the site running and to spot abuse.
  • Google Fonts and Material Symbols. The site loads fonts and icons from Google's CDN. When your browser requests these files, Google may log your IP address. We don't receive anything additional from Google about you.

What we don't collect

  • We do not use advertising cookies or retargeting pixels on this site.
  • We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

How we use your information

We use what you give us for a short list of operational purposes:

  • Responding to your inquiry and preparing a quote.
  • Scheduling and performing the job.
  • Sending invoices, receipts, and follow-up.
  • Asking for a review after the job is done (only if you opted in or gave permission).
  • Complying with our legal and accounting obligations.
  • Protecting the site from fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

We don't send marketing emails to people who haven't asked for them.

Who we share your information with

We share information with a small number of service providers who help us run the business. These providers process your data on our behalf and are not allowed to use it for their own purposes.

  • Formspree processes our contact-form submissions and forwards them to our inbox. Their privacy policy is at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Google (Gmail) hosts our business email. If you email us, your message is stored on Google's servers subject to their privacy policy.
  • Our web hosting provider serves this site and maintains access and error logs as described above.

We may also disclose information when required by law (for example, a valid subpoena), to protect the safety of people or property, or if the business is ever sold or merged. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep contact inquiries and job records for as long as needed to service the job and to meet legal, tax, and insurance recordkeeping requirements, typically up to seven years. After that we delete or anonymize the data.

Your rights (California residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act and the California Privacy Rights Act, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have about you.
  • Request a copy of that information.
  • Ask us to delete it (with some exceptions for records we're required to keep).
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email Junkawaywego@gmail.com or call 760-473-3973. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We aim to respond within 45 days.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) across this site. No method is 100% secure, so we don't claim to be. If we ever suffer a security incident affecting your information, we'll notify you as required by law.

Children

This site is directed at adults seeking junk removal and demolition services. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

"Do Not Track" and global privacy signals

Because we don't use tracking cookies or targeted advertising, we don't need to honor Do Not Track signals specifically. We also don't sell or share your data for cross-context behavioral advertising, so Global Privacy Control signals don't change how we handle your data.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the effective date at the top. Material changes will also be flagged on the homepage for at least 30 days.
